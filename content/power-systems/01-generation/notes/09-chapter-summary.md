### Formulas

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Line loss for the same power | $\propto 1/V^2$ |
| Average load | energy ÷ hours (year = 8760 h) |
| Demand factor | MD ÷ connected load (≤ 1) |
| Load factor | average load ÷ MD (≤ 1) |
| Diversity factor | Σ individual MDs ÷ MD of the group (≥ 1) |
| Capacity factor | average load ÷ plant capacity = LF × utilisation factor |
| Utilisation factor | MD ÷ plant capacity |
| Reserve capacity | capacity − MD |
| Coal per hour | $P(\text{kW})\times860\,/\,(\eta_{overall}\times CV)$ kg |
| Overall efficiency | thermal η × generator η |
| Hydro power | $9.81\,Q\,H\,\eta$ kW |
| Specific speed | $N\sqrt P/H^{5/4}$ |
| Wind power | $\tfrac12\rho AV^3$; Betz limit 16/27 = 59.3 % |
| Fill factor | $V_{mp}I_{mp}/(V_{oc}I_{sc})$ |
| Cost per kWh | $F/(P_m\times LF\times8760) + c$ |
| Straight-line depreciation | (cost − salvage) ÷ life |

### One-line facts that are asked again and again

- Power system = generating stations + transmission + distribution. Main job: convert energy and transmit it with maximum efficiency.
- Grid: less reserve, diversity, better hydro-thermal co-ordination, better frequency control, **reliability** (supply without failure over long periods).
- Feed pump develops the cycle pressure. Superheater raises steam temperature. Economiser heats feed water with **flue gases**. Air preheater heats air. **ESP collects dust from flue gases.** Condenser keeps vacuum.
- Supercritical: above 221 bar; higher efficiency; emissions reduced (never "completely eliminated").
- Hydro output depends on **head, discharge and efficiency**. Surge tank stops water hammer. Pondage **increases firm capacity**.
- Pelton: impulse, high head. Francis: reaction, medium head. **Kaplan: reaction, low varying head, adjustable runner blades.** Reaction turbine = runner fully in water, driven by pressure drop.
- Moderator slows neutrons (heavy water, graphite); **control rods absorb neutrons (boron, cadmium)**.
- **Base load:** nuclear, coal, run-of-river. **Peak load:** gas turbine, diesel, pumped storage, storage hydro. Wind farms are neither.
- Nuclear is not renewable. Wind is the least reliable renewable; tip speed ratio is its key parameter; drag type = low speed, high torque.
- PV: dark current = reverse saturation current. More power: $I_L$ large, $R_s$ small, $R_{sh}$ large.
- Fixed + semi-fixed costs = **standing charges**. High load factor and high diversity factor → cheaper energy.
- Two-part tariff = ₹/kW of maximum demand + ₹/kWh.

### Traps

1. Power factor is not used in the load factor (kW basis).
2. Diversity factor is ≥ 1; demand and load factors are ≤ 1.
3. Reservoir area is not needed for hydro *power*.
4. "Reaction turbines suit high head, low flow": false (that is Pelton).
5. "Same capacity meets **lower** demand" in a grid: wrong way round.
6. Coal per hour: use the **overall** efficiency (thermal × generator), not one of them alone.
7. Specific speed has $H^{5/4}$ (1.25) in the denominator, not $H^{2.25}$.
